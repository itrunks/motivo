## Current variant matrix

Tata.ev's current price page lists Smart 19, Pure+ 19, Pure+ 24 and Creative+ 24. The 19.2 kWh battery is paired with a 45 kW PMSM, 110 Nm and 226 km MIDC range. The 24 kWh battery is paired with a 55 kW PMSM, 114 Nm and 285 km MIDC range. All variants are front-wheel drive, automatic and 5-seat hatchbacks.

## Price observations

Autocar India's May 28, 2026 facelift price table lists Smart 19 ₹6,99,000; Pure+ 19 ₹8,49,000; Pure+ 24 ₹9,49,000; Creative+ 24 ₹9,99,000. These are secondary-source ex-showroom observations. Tata's current product page confirms the Smart/Pure+/Creative+ variant names and that prices are ex-showroom, but its extracted page does not show numeric variant prices. BaaS for Smart 19 is ₹4.69 lakh upfront plus ₹2.60/km and is a separate commercial basis.
