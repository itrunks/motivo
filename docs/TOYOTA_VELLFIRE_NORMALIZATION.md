## Current grade and powertrain structure

Toyota's official India brochure and 2023 launch release list two grades: Hi and VIP Executive Lounge. Both use Toyota's 2.5-litre self-charging strong-hybrid system, e-CVT transmission and 7-seat cabin. The brochure specifies a 2.5-litre hybrid engine, 142 kW (193 PS) engine output and continuously variable transmission. Body style is MPV.

## Price evidence

Toyota's current official model-range listing shows Vellfire from ₹1,19,99,000. The current showroom page says the grade price is not available and directs customers to the nearest dealer. The 2023 launch release lists Hi at ₹1,19,90,000 and VIP Executive Lounge at ₹1,29,90,000. These are historical launch prices and are not represented as current grade-wise prices. No current VIP price was captured.
