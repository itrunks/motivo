## Current India configuration

Toyota's current India brochure and showroom list two grades: ZX and GR-S. Both use the 3.3-litre V6 twin-turbo diesel engine, 10-speed automatic transmission, AWD and 5-seat cabin. The GR-S grade has off-road tuned suspension and front/rear differential locks. The vehicle is a full-size SUV.

## Price observations and discrepancy

Toyota's February 19, 2025 booking announcement lists ZX at ₹2,31,00,000 and GR-S at ₹2,41,00,000 ex-showroom all-India. Toyota's current official model-range listing shows Land Cruiser 300 from ₹2,17,75,000, which is lower than the 2025 ZX launch price and does not identify the grade. Both observations are retained separately; the current listing is not mapped to ZX or GR-S. Obtain current dealer grade-wise quote before customer use.
