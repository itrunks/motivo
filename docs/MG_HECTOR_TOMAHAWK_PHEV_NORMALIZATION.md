## Current variant structure

MG's official launch matrix lists Exclusive 7S and Essence 7S for the Hector Tomahawk PHEV. Both use a 20.5 kWh plug-in hybrid battery with a 1.5 l petrol engine and automatic operation. The official page states over 115 km pure-electric range and 1,100+ km combined range. The PHEV is 7-seater only. Body style is SUV.

## Price observations

August 26, 2026 official launch prices: Exclusive 7S ₹25,69,800 and Essence 7S ₹27,79,800 ex-showroom. BaaS is a separate ₹21.79 lakh upfront plus ₹3.20/km battery rental and is not mixed into the standard price file.
