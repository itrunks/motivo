## Current orderable Series X lineup

The current official Tata.ev price page lists Accomplished X 55, Empowered X 55 and Empowered X 55 #DARK. All use a 55 kWh LFP battery, 123 kW / 167 PS PMSM, 215 Nm, front-wheel drive and automatic transmission. Certified MIDC range is 502 km. The vehicle is a 5-seat SUV coupé.

## Price observations

Official Tata.ev FAQ values: Accomplished X 55 ₹16,99,000; Empowered X 55 ₹19,19,000; Empowered X 55 #DARK ₹19,49,000. These are ex-showroom prices; current dealer invoice price may vary.

## Historical series separation

Older 45 kWh and 55 kWh Curvv.ev variants (Creative, Accomplished, Accomplished+ S, Empowered+ etc.) appear in earlier brochure/third-party material. The current official price page now labels the lineup Series X and lists the three configurations above. Older 45 kWh grades are not mixed into the current Series X catalogue without current configurator proof of orderability.
