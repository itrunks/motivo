## Current variant structure

The official MG Astor 2025/2026 brochure lists one 1.5 l VTi-TECH petrol engine (1498 cc), paired as follows:
- Sprint: 5MT
- Shine: 5MT
- Select: 5MT / 8CVT
- Sharp Pro: 5MT / 8CVT
- Savvy Pro: 8CVT

Total: 7 combinations. The current MG product configurator still exposes Astor and a selected Savvy Pro CVT configuration. The vehicle is a 5-seat SUV. The 2026 brochure states 110 PS and 144 Nm.

## Price observations

Official brochure prices effective 2 January 2026: Sprint MT ₹9,79,100; Shine MT ₹11,34,100; Select MT ₹12,40,100; Select CVT ₹13,56,100; Sharp Pro MT ₹13,37,100; Sharp Pro CVT ₹14,52,100; Savvy Pro CVT ₹15,30,100. These are ex-showroom prices; dual-tone paint adds a separate charge and is not represented as a mechanical variant.

## Availability caveat

Secondary reporting in October/November 2026 says dealers may have stopped taking new Astor orders, but MG has not published an official discontinuation notice. MG's official product page and configurator still show the model and booking action. Therefore the catalogue records the official listed variants, but new-order availability must be confirmed with an MG dealer before presenting the vehicle as orderable.
