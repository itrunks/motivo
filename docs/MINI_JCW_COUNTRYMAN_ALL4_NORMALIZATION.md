## Current India configuration

MINI India lists the John Cooper Works Countryman ALL4 as the high-performance petrol Countryman. The official MINI product page states 300 HP and 400 Nm. The India configuration uses a 2.0-litre TwinPower Turbo four-cylinder petrol engine, 1998 cc, 7-speed DCT and ALL4 all-wheel drive. It is a 5-seat performance SUV.

## Price basis

Autocar India's Countryman variant listing shows the JCW ALL4 at **₹77.07 lakh on-road Delhi**. MINI India's official home page lists a base price of ₹66.15 lakh; that is a separate manufacturer-listed price basis. Do not confuse the two.
