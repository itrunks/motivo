## Current India variants

Autocar India's variant page was last updated 16 October 2026 and lists Cooper Convertible S, Cooper Convertible S JCW Pack and Cooper Convertible Stardust Edition.

All three use a 2.0-litre TwinPower Turbo four-cylinder petrol engine, 1998 cc, 204 PS, 300 Nm and a 7-speed DCT. Drive is front-wheel drive and seating capacity is four. The JCW Pack and Stardust Edition remain separate retail edition labels; they are not separate engine families.

MINI India's official product page confirms 204 PS and four-seat configuration. The official brochure also confirms the 7-speed dual-clutch transmission.

## Price basis

MINI India's December 2025 official S specification sheet and live product page list ₹58,50,000 ex-showroom for Cooper Convertible S. The official page FAQ says both Cooper and Cooper S performance levels exist, but only S is detailed in the retrieved specification sheet. Do not copy the S price to the base Cooper level or the special editions.

Autocar India lists three **on-road Delhi INR** observations: ₹68.74 lakh, ₹71.65 lakh and ₹73.28 lakh for S, S JCW Pack and Stardust Edition respectively. These are secondary-source city-specific prices, not ex-showroom prices and must not be reused as national or Tamil Nadu prices.

## Current MINI India page recheck — 2026-11-07

The current MINI India site now provides three relevant product pages:

1. **MINI Cooper Convertible** — ₹58,50,000 displayed; shown model is Favoured Trim, with 204 PS, 150 kW, 237 km/h top speed, 0–100 km/h in 6.9 s and four seats.
2. **MINI Convertible S JCW Pack** — starting from ₹58,50,000, 204 hp. MINI describes JCW as a style/equipment package, not a separate engine family.
3. **MINI Convertible Stardust Edition** — current official edition page, 204 hp and 300 Nm; no price is displayed in the captured page.

The official product FAQ states that Classic, Favoured and JCW are optional style/equipment packages. It also says there are two performance levels, Cooper and Cooper S, both using a 2.0-litre TwinPower Turbo four-cylinder petrol engine. However, the current India product page and retrieved specification sheet provide detailed numeric specifications only for the S configuration. Do not infer a separate India price or exact output for a base Cooper performance level from this generic FAQ sentence.

The MINI India S specification sheet confirms 1998 cc, 204 hp (150 kW), 300 Nm and 7-speed Steptronic dual-clutch transmission. The official current product page confirms four seats. Drive type is not stated in the retrieved official pages and remains blank.

The official S price is now rechecked on the live MINI product page at ₹58,50,000. The JCW Pack page also states a starting price from ₹58,50,000. The Stardust Edition page has no displayed price, so the Autocar ₹73.28L on-road Delhi observation remains secondary and must not be presented as official ex-showroom. The official model page does not assign its ₹58.50L amount to Stardust.
