## Current India variants

Toyota's July 28, 2026 launch announcement and current showroom engine/trim plan list:
- GX 4x2 AT — ₹31,99,000
- GX 4x4 AT — ₹33,69,000
- VX 4x4 AT — ₹36,69,000

All three use the 2.8 l BS6 Phase 2 diesel engine, 6-speed automatic transmission and 5-seat cabin. The 4x4 grades include H4/L4, electronic differential lock and downhill assist; GX 4x2 is 2WD. Body style is recorded as pickup truck based on the official Hilux positioning and utility deck configuration.

## Current model update versus older workqueue

The workqueue contains an older Hilux observation with 6MT and 6AT and 4WD only. The July 2026 all-new Hilux India launch replaces that captured lineup with three automatic grades, including GX 4x2. The older manual/4WD observations are not carried forward as current retail variants.

## Price basis
