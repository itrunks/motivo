## Current variant structure

The current MG India product page lists Excite, Exclusive, Essence, Exclusive Pro and Essence Pro. The October 14, 2026 newsroom release adds the INDULGE Edition as a current special edition.

All configurations use a Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor, automatic transmission, 5 seats and 100 kW / 136 PS with 200 Nm. The 38 kWh battery is used by Excite, Exclusive, Essence and INDULGE Edition, with 332 km certified range. Exclusive Pro and Essence Pro use 52.9 kWh battery, with 449 km certified range. Body classification is CUV (Crossover Utility Vehicle), as MG positions the Windsor.

## Price observations

Official product cards: Excite ₹14,84,800; Exclusive ₹16,14,800; Essence ₹17,14,800; Exclusive Pro ₹18,04,800; Essence Pro ₹19,14,800. The INDULGE Edition was launched on October 14, 2026 at ₹17,99,800. BaaS prices are lower upfront amounts plus battery rental per kilometre and are not mixed into the standard-purchase price CSV.

The current page's FAQ gives a narrower standard Windsor price range and does not include Pro prices in that sentence; the individual product cards provide the grade-specific figures used in the CSV. Effective dates for regular card prices are not stated.
