## Current battery and grade structure

Toyota's January 20, 2026 launch announcement and official brochure list three grades:
- E1: 49 kWh LFP battery, 106 kW motor, 189 Nm.
- E2: 61 kWh LFP battery, 128 kW motor, 189 Nm.
- E3: 61 kWh LFP battery, 128 kW motor, 193 Nm.

All grades use front-wheel drive, automatic electric transmission and a 5-seat cabin. The body style is SUV. The current showroom page lists the E3 61 kWh specification and a 543 km ARAI-certified range; its FAQ confirms LFP battery chemistry and that no petrol or hybrid variants are planned.

## Price

Toyota's May 29, 2026 official price announcement lists E3 at ₹23,60,000 ex-showroom. The price announcement does not provide E1 or E2 prices, so those remain blank. Toyota's model range page also lists Urban Cruiser Ebella from ₹23,60,000, consistent with E3. No E1/E2 price is inferred.
