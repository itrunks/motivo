## Current variant structure

MG's official EV page lists Excite 7S, Exclusive 7S, Essence 5S and Essence 7S. All use a 69.2 kWh LFP MAGIC battery, single electric motor, automatic transmission, 204 PS and 310 Nm. Claimed certified range is up to 517 km. The 7.4 kW AC home charger is included; 90 kW DC charging takes 30–80% in about 35 minutes. Body style is SUV.

## Price observations

The August 26, 2026 official launch release gives full-ownership ex-showroom prices: Excite 7S ₹19,49,800; Exclusive 7S ₹21,99,800; Essence 5S ₹22,99,800; Essence 7S ₹23,49,800. The BaaS offer (₹13.99 lakh plus ₹4.90/km) is a different price basis and is not mixed into the standard price CSV.
