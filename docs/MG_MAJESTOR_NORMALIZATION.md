## Current powertrain families

MG's February 12, 2026 official launch announcement lists Sharp and Savvy grades across 2WD and 4WD. All use a 2.0-litre twin-turbo diesel engine, 8-speed automatic transmission, 215.5 PS and 478.5 Nm. The 4WD system includes front, rear and centre differential locks, 10 off-road modes and crawl control. The model is a D+ segment SUV. MG's release describes 6- and 7-seat configurations at model level but does not map exact seat count to each Sharp/Savvy and 2WD/4WD combination; those are therefore left as options, not split into invented rows.

## Price discrepancy

MG's national homepage currently displays Majestor starting at ₹43,49,000. An MG Chennai dealer page displays starting at ₹45,99,000. Neither source maps the starting price to a specific Sharp/Savvy or 2WD/4WD configuration. Both are retained as separate model-level observations; current grade-wise prices and effective date require confirmation from the live configurator/dealer.
