## Current official evidence

Audi India currently promotes the **New Audi Q7 SUV** with pre-booking. The teaser page reports up to 340 hp / 250 kW, up to 500 Nm, 0–100 km/h in 5.6 seconds and seating for up to seven. The current Q7 model page confirms standard quattro all-wheel drive and describes the third row as an option.

The current product pages do not expose a grade/trim list, engine-by-grade matrix or numeric ex-showroom price. Audi's official Price Reckoner is available but its embedded values do not render in the captured page text. No third-party price is substituted.

## Historical specification cross-check

The 2024 Audi Q7 brochure identifies a 3.0L TFSI 48V mild-hybrid engine, 2,995 cc, 340 hp / 250 kW, 500 Nm, quattro AWD and 5.6-second 0–100 km/h. This is consistent with the current model-level performance figures but remains a historical brochure; it is not used to infer the new model's full grade matrix.
