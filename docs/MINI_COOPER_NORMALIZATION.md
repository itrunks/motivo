Checked: 2026-11-06

- `data/mini-cooper-variant-structure.csv`: 3 India-listed 3-door petrol retail labels with engine, gearbox, drivetrain and seating.

- `data/mini-cooper-current-structure-observations.csv` and `data/mini-cooper-historical-price-observations.csv`: current model-page specification and separately dated February 2024 specification/price evidence.

## Current India variants

Autocar India's variant page was last updated 28 October 2026 and lists Cooper S 2.0 Petrol, Cooper S Oxford Edition 2.0 Petrol and Cooper S JCW 2.0 Petrol.

All three are recorded with the 2.0-litre TwinPower Turbo four-cylinder petrol engine, 1998 cc, 204 PS, 300 Nm, 7-speed DCT and front-wheel drive. The model is a 4-seat 3-door hatchback. Edition/appearance packages are kept as distinct retail labels; they are not treated as separate engine configurations.

## Official source reconciliation

The current MINI India product page reports 204 hp / 150 kW, 242 km/h top speed and 0–100 km/h in 6.6 seconds, but does not expose a current numeric variant-price matrix in the captured page text. The official February 2024 S specification sheet is retained separately as historical evidence: 1,998 cc, 204 hp / 150 kW, 300 Nm, 7-speed DCT and ₹44,45,000 ex-showroom. Its Classic/Favoured pack surcharges are not treated as current prices. The current MINI page also promotes the Oxford Edition and Cooper S JCW Pack; the three current retail labels in the secondary variant list are preserved separately.

## Price basis

All three prices are **on-road Delhi INR** from Autocar India's current variant page: ₹51.79 lakh, ₹64.66 lakh and ₹64.84 lakh. These are not ex-showroom prices and must not be reused as national or Tamil Nadu prices.
