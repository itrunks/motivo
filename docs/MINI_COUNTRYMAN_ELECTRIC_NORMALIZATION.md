## Current India variants

Autocar India's variant page was last updated 23 October 2026 and lists four variants: Essential EV, Classic Pack EV, Favoured Pack EV and SE ALL4.

The first three use the single-motor Countryman E powertrain: 66.45 kWh gross battery, 204 PS, 250 Nm, front-wheel drive and 462 km WLTP range. SE ALL4 uses dual motors, 313 PS, 494 Nm, all-wheel drive and 433 km WLTP range. All are 5-seat electric SUVs with automatic single-speed drive.

## Price basis

Autocar India's on-road Delhi observations are ₹58.47 lakh (Essential EV), ₹61.08 lakh (Classic Pack EV), ₹63.85 lakh (Favoured Pack EV) and ₹71.25 lakh (SE ALL4). These are on-road Delhi prices, not ex-showroom or Tamil Nadu quotes. MINI's official India range pages and specification sheets support the powertrain details; the Autocar page provides the current four-trim price matrix.
