## Current powertrain and grade structure

Toyota's June 4, 2026 announcement lists GX, GX Fleet, GX+, VX and ZX. All are powered by a 2.4-litre diesel engine with manual transmission. Seating: GX, GX Fleet, GX+ and VX are offered in 7- and 8-seat layouts; ZX is 7-seat only. The Toyota showroom specification lists 5-speed manual and 2,393 cc displacement. Body style is MPV.

The November 5, 2026 Toyota release creates Innova Crysta LIMO as a dedicated fleet identity. It states GX Fleet 7S and 8S prices only. GX Fleet remains a separate sales-channel row from retail GX; LIMO is not added as a separate passenger trim.

## Price observations and date scope

June 4, 2026 official launch prices: GX 7S ₹19,72,000 / 8S ₹19,77,000; GX Fleet 7S ₹19,72,000 / 8S ₹19,77,000; GX+ 7S ₹21,15,000 / 8S ₹21,20,000; VX 7S ₹24,93,000 / 8S ₹24,98,000; ZX 7S ₹26,63,000.

November 5, 2026 official fleet update: GX Fleet 7S ₹19,95,000 and 8S ₹19,99,000. These later values supersede the June fleet prices only. The current Toyota showroom still displays a starting price of ₹19,72,000, while third-party reports on November 4 describe a retail price revision. Retail post-November prices are not treated as verified official values here; obtain current dealer quote before customer use.

## Reconciliation notes

The current online showroom specification page still shows an older grade G and lacks the 2026 GX Fleet/GX+ grade naming, while the June 2026 launch announcement is the newer source for current grades. The older showroom is not used to introduce the obsolete G grade into the current 2026 lineup. Model-year remains blank because no formal per-variant model-year is supplied.
