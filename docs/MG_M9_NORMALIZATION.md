## Current configuration

MG SELECT's M9 is a 90 kWh NMC battery electric MPV with a single electric motor, 245 PS, 350 Nm, automatic transmission and a 7-seat cabin. The official India launch announcement states 548 km MIDC range. The Couture Edition retains the M9 powertrain and adds a couture-themed exterior/interior treatment; it is recorded separately as a special edition, not as a new mechanical variant.

## Price basis

- Standard M9: ₹69,90,000 introductory full-ownership price from July 21, 2025. This is historical; current full-ownership price is not verified.
- Standard M9 BaaS: ₹69.99 lakh upfront plus ₹8.00/km battery rental, introduced September 17, 2026.
- M9 Couture Edition: ₹84,94,000, announced August 3, 2026.

These three price bases are not merged. The Couture Edition price is not used as the standard M9 price.
