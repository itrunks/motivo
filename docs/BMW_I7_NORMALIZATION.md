# BMW i7 India normalization

Checked: 2026-11-06

BMW India launched the updated i7 range in September 2026. Two India-market electric configurations are recorded: i7 50 xDrive M Sport and i7 M70 xDrive. The i7 50 is locally produced; the M70 is a completely built-up unit.

| Variant | System output | Torque | Battery | WLTP range | 0-100 km/h | India launch price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| i7 50 xDrive M Sport | 455 hp | 660 Nm | 112.5 kWh | 728 km | 5.5 s | INR 1,95,00,000 |
| i7 M70 xDrive | 680 hp | 1,015 Nm; up to 1,100 Nm boost | Not stated | 686 km | 3.8 s | INR 2,65,00,000 |

Both have dual electric motors, xDrive AWD, 250 kW DC and 22 kW AC charging. The M70 battery capacity is not stated in the India launch release. The M70 price is sourced from the dated official launch release; the current catalogue separately shows the i7 50 at INR 1,95,00,000.
