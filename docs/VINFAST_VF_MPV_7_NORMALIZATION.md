## Current configuration

VinFast India offers the VF MPV 7 as a single fully loaded configuration. It is a 7-seat electric MPV with a 60.13 kWh usable battery, one front-mounted electric motor, 150 kW / 204 PS, 280 Nm, front-wheel drive and 517 km ARAI range. The official launch release confirms the seven-seat layout, 150 kW output, 280 Nm and 517 km ARAI range; the current official product page shows the same battery capacity and range.

## Price revision

The current official VinFast India product page displays ₹20,39,000 ex-showroom. Autocar India reported on 4 November 2026 that this is a ₹4.10 lakh reduction from the 15 April 2026 launch price of ₹24,49,000. Both values are retained in the price file with their respective effective dates; the launch amount is historical and must not be treated as current.
