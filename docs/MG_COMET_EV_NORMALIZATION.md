## Current structure

The current MG India product page lists Executive, Excite, Excite FC, Exclusive and Exclusive FC. All use a Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor, automatic transmission, 4 seats, 42 PS and 110 Nm. Standard variants use 17.3 kWh battery and 3.3 kW AC charging; FC variants use 17.4 kWh and 7.4 kW AC charging. Claimed range is 230 km. Body style is hatchback.

## Prices

Executive ₹7,79,800; Excite ₹8,89,800; Excite FC ₹9,16,800; Exclusive ₹9,79,800; Exclusive FC ₹10,06,800. These are standard-purchase ex-showroom observations. BaaS prices are separate upfront prices plus ₹3.20/km battery rental and are not mixed into the standard price file. Effective date is not stated.

Blackstorm is absent from the five current variants on the current official page and is not included as a current retail trim.
