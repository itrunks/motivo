## Current variant matrix

Official 2026 launch matrix: Smart 30, Smart+ 30, Smart+ 40, Adventure 40, Empowered 40 and Empowered+ S 40. All use front-mounted PMSM, FWD, automatic transmission and 5 seats. The 30 kWh pack produces 65 kW / 154 Nm and 365–375 km MIDC range. The 40 kWh pack produces 95 kW / 154 Nm and 468 km MIDC range. Body style is SUV.
