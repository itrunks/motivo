## Current variants

VinFast India lists Earth, Wind and Wind Infinity. All three use a 59.6 kWh battery and a single front-mounted electric motor with front-wheel drive. Earth is rated at 130 kW / 177 PS and 250 Nm with 468 km ARAI range. Wind and Wind Infinity are rated at 150 kW / 204 PS and 310 Nm with 463 km ARAI range. Wind Infinity is an equipment/roof package on the Wind powertrain, not a separate battery or motor configuration. All are 5-seat compact electric SUVs.

## Price basis

The official VF 6 product page and reservation portal display ₹18,19,000 as the model starting ex-showroom price. The current official pages captured do not provide a complete trim-wise price matrix, so this is not assigned to each of the three grades. The January 2026 third-party report contains variant-wise prices (Earth ₹17.29 lakh, Wind ₹18.69 lakh, Wind Infinity ₹19.19 lakh), but these are not treated as current official prices because the official page does not confirm the individual trim prices or effective date.
