**Audit date:** 2026-11-06

## Executive outcome

Maserati's India range was refreshed and formally relaunched on **30 October 2026** with the updated Grecale, GranTurismo and GranCabrio, plus the India debut of GT2 Stradale. The official Stellantis/Maserati release confirms the refreshed range and Navnit Maserati partnership, but does not provide a full India price list. The official Maserati India site and configurator provide useful model and some starting-price evidence.

- Current India retail candidates: **Grecale, Grecale Folgore, GranTurismo, GranCabrio, MCPURA, MCPURA Cielo and GT2 Stradale**.
- Official Maserati India website lists Grecale, GranTurismo, GranCabrio, MCPURA, MCPURA Cielo and GT2 Stradale in its model navigation.
- The official India site currently shows Grecale starting prices by derivative: ₹1.14 Cr, ₹1.29 Cr, ₹1.39 Cr, ₹2.00 Cr and ₹1.90 Cr (Folgore), with a second configurator view showing Modena at ₹1.41 Cr. Preserve the conflicting observations; do not silently reconcile.
- Secondary reporting states refreshed GranTurismo starts at ₹2.62 Cr and GranCabrio at ₹2.91 Cr; variants reported at ₹2.80 Cr / ₹3.10 Cr for Trofeo. Treat as secondary price evidence.
- GT2 Stradale made its India debut at the 30 October event; India retail price and orderability are not confirmed by the OEM announcement.
- MCPURA and MCPURA Cielo appear on official India site/configurator, but configurator labels the models as 2027. Verify India delivery/order status before treating as immediately orderable.
- Do not include Levante, Quattroporte, MC20 or legacy Ghibli merely because aggregators retain historical price cards.

## Official model identity and specifications

The official India configurator identifies:
- Grecale (2026): 2.0L L4 mild hybrid, 300 PS; AWD.
- Grecale Modena (2026): 2.0L L4 mild hybrid, 330 PS; AWD.
- Grecale Trofeo (2026): 3.0L V6, 530 PS; AWD.
- Grecale Folgore: 550 PS electric; AWD.
- GranTurismo: V6 petrol 490 PS; AWD.
- GranTurismo Trofeo: V6 petrol 550 PS; AWD.
- GranTurismo Folgore: 761 PS electric; AWD.
- GranCabrio: V6 petrol 490 PS; AWD.
- GranCabrio Trofeo: V6 petrol 550 PS; AWD.
- GranCabrio Folgore: 761 PS electric; AWD.
- MCPURA and MCPURA Cielo: 3.0L V6 petrol, 630 PS; RWD.
- GT2 Stradale: 3.0L V6 petrol, 640 PS; RWD.

Configurator metadata labels MCPURA, MCPURA Cielo and GT2 Stradale as model year 2027. This is not by itself proof of Indian delivery availability.

## Exclusions / lifecycle watchlist

- **Levante, Quattroporte, Ghibli:** legacy models visible in some aggregator listings; not present in current official India model navigation/configurator set reviewed here. Exclude pending dealer proof of current new-car orderability.
- **MC20:** legacy/previous-generation model not present in current official India configurator range; do not include as current without dealer confirmation.
- **MCXtrema / Maserati GT2:** track-only products, not road-registered retail cars; exclude from passenger-car catalogue.
- **Upcoming / model-year 2027:** MCPURA family and GT2 Stradale need India orderability confirmation despite official site presence.
