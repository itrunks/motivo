## Exact official facts captured

### New City
Official page currently calls it "The New Honda City 2026". It states:
- Petrol grades: SV, V, ZX and ZX+ with MT; V, ZX and ZX+ with CVT.
- e:HEV grade: ZX+.
- Petrol transmission: 6-speed MT or CVT.
- e:HEV transmission: e-CVT.
- Official homepage starting price: ₹11,99,900 (homepage marker uses a footnote; price type/city scope must be confirmed from the linked price page before import).

### New Amaze
Official FAQ states:
- Petrol only; no diesel.
- Manual and CVT.
- Four trim names are described in the FAQ text as V, VX and ZX (three unique grade names; retain source wording and verify exact transmission-by-grade matrix before import).
- Starting price ₹7,65,400, explicitly described as ex-showroom Delhi.

### New Elevate
Official press release dated 2026-10-01 confirms pre-launch bookings opened nationwide ahead of the 2026-10-06 launch. The official homepage now shows New Elevate from ₹11,80,000. The October 6, 2026 launch release and current brochure should be used to validate refreshed variant naming and equipment changes; do not reuse 2023/2025 trim data without checking the 2026 refresh.

### All New ZR-V
The OEM brochure index includes "All New ZR-V" and the owner's manual index includes "ZR-V e:HEV (2026~) Emergency Rescue Method Manual". Homepage lists the price as ₹49,99,900. Treat it as an imported hybrid model; confirm grade count, exact price type, and booking/orderability before importing.
