# MINI India pilot audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** India  
**Decision:** Current brand; current model families identified, but only one current OEM-backed price observation accepted provisionally. Do not treat the global MINI configurator's displayed tax language as an India price list.

## Current India range

The official MINI India site currently promotes the Countryman C, Oxford Edition, Convertible Stardust Edition, Cooper S JCW Pack, GP Inspired, Convertible, JCW Countryman ALL4, Cooper and all-electric Countryman. The official BMW Group India newsroom confirms the locally manufactured MINI Countryman C launched in India and bookings opened through authorised MINI dealerships.

Canonical catalogue candidates:
- MINI Cooper / Cooper S (hatch family; map special packs/editions to the correct generation and derivative only after India orderability is confirmed).
- MINI Cooper Convertible (including special editions as derivatives, not separate model families).
- MINI Countryman C (petrol, locally manufactured).
- MINI Countryman Electric (BEV; preserve battery/powertrain derivative).
- MINI JCW Countryman ALL4 (performance derivative; verify current allocation/orderability).

Do not assume every promotional tile is a separately orderable variant. MINI Oxford Edition, Convertible Stardust Edition, Cooper S JCW Pack and GP Inspired require current India booking/configurator confirmation and canonical derivative mapping.
