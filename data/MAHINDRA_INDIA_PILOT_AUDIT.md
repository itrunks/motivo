## Price evidence and import boundary

The official SUV catalogue page shows model-level price ranges for 11 passenger SUVs. These are not variant-level price observations and must not be expanded into fabricated trim prices. The XUV 3XO ZNG, Thar OG and REVX EDGE press releases provide exact OEM-published edition/model starting prices; preserve their edition context and price footnotes, and do not assume they represent a full price list.

Third-party November 2026 catalogues disagree on some current price ranges, notably XUV 7XO and EV models. Do not resolve such conflicts by averaging or selecting a third-party number over the OEM catalogue. Capture dated OEM variant price lists/configurator evidence for each exact trim.

On-road prices must be separate city/RTO observations. No city-wise on-road price list was accepted in this pass.

## Scope and classification rules

- Keep passenger SUVs separate from commercial pickups.
- Keep Thar OG and Thar ROXX as separate model families.
- Keep XUV 3XO ICE and XUV 3XO EV separate.
- Keep XUV400 separate from XUV 3XO EV and the Electric Origin SUV family.
- Keep editions (ZNG, REVX EDGE) as edition/variant-level records under the correct base model only after configurator/brochure verification.
- Treat upcoming concept vehicles and announced future products as non-orderable until official booking/price/variant evidence exists.
