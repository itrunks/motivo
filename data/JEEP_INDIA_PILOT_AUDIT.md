## Dated OEM evidence captured

### Compass Sport 9-speed automatic diesel — 2026-10-26

Jeep India announced a new Compass Sport FWD with a 9-speed automatic transmission on 2026-10-26. The press release calls it the most affordable automatic Compass offered in India. This is a current variant-family addition. The exact price was not present in the captured release content; do not infer it from other Compass automatic trims or third-party price listings.

Official source:
https://www.media.stellantis.com/in-en/jeep/press

### Wrangler Rubicon Whitecap Edition — 2026-10-13

Jeep India announced that all 25 India-allocated Wrangler Rubicon Whitecap Edition units had sold out ahead of launch. Keep the edition in historical/sold-out special-edition observations, not the current available-to-order variant list.

Official source:
https://www.media.stellantis.com/in-en/jeep/press

### Compass 85th Anniversary Edition — 2026-08-17

Official Jeep release states that the Compass 85th Anniversary Edition is limited to 85 units across India. Preserve this as a limited-edition product/variant record, not a separate model. Current allocation status and exact current price require official price/configurator confirmation.

Official source:
https://www.media.stellantis.com/in-en/jeep/press

### Meridian 85th Anniversary Edition — 2026-07-24

Official release announces a Meridian 85th Anniversary Edition with a production run limited to 85 units. Keep it as a limited-edition Meridian variant; confirm allocation and price before marking orderable.

Official source:
https://www.media.stellantis.com/in-en/jeep/press

### 2026 Meridian model-year update — 2026-01-21

Official Jeep release confirms the 2026 Meridian retains the 2.0-litre turbo diesel engine producing 170hp and 350Nm, with 6MT/9AT and 4x4 availability. Sliding second-row seats are introduced on select Limited and Overland variants. The release does not provide a complete price matrix.

Official source:
https://www.jeep-india.com/content/dam/cross-regional/apac/jeep/en_in/press-release/Jeep-india-introduces-new-2026-meridian-with-sliding-second-row-seats-for-enhanced-comfort.pdf
