## Executive outcome

The India retail catalogue currently contains four model families: **Ghost, Phantom, Cullinan and Spectre**. The official Rolls-Royce newsroom confirms the brand's renewed India commitment with a reimagined Chennai showroom opened in October 2026. The official newsroom also reports the global public-road debut of Spectre Series II in October 2026.

India launch timing for Spectre Series II is reported as by December 2026, but it is not yet an established current India retail model on the audit date. Keep it in the upcoming watchlist, not in the currently orderable catalogue, until an India launch / order guide is published.

Rolls-Royce prices are especially sensitive to specification and Bespoke commissions. Treat public price listings as starting-price observations, not fixed transaction prices. The sources also conflict materially on Phantom: Autocar India lists ₹12.87–15.07 crore, while ZigWheels lists ₹8.99–10.48 crore. Do not reconcile these without a dated dealer quote and exact model/derivative mapping.

## India market update — Spectre Series II

- Rolls-Royce official newsroom confirms Spectre Series II made its public-road debut in Stockholm on 23 October 2026. This is a global product milestone, not an India retail launch.
- HT Auto reported on 22 October 2026 that Rolls-Royce expects Spectre Series II to arrive in India by the end of 2026. The report cites an official source but the exact India price was not announced.
- Current Spectre Series II global figures reported by HT Auto: 112 kWh battery, dual-motor AWD, 601 hp / 1,015 Nm standard, 680 hp / 1,110 Nm Black Badge in Spirited mode, claimed WLTP range 628–629 km.
- Keep Spectre Series II as an upcoming India derivative until the company publishes India launch and order information. Do not overwrite current Spectre price records with estimates.

## India retail network

- Rolls-Royce Motor Cars official newsroom announced the opening of its reimagined Chennai showroom on 22 October 2026. This is an official brand announcement and confirms a new South India retail presence.
- ZigWheels dealer directory checked on 5 November 2026 lists three authorised showrooms in Chennai, Delhi and Hyderabad. Treat the city count as secondary directory data; confirm dealer names and current addresses through the official dealer locator before displaying them in the public app.
- HT Auto reported that the Delhi showroom is expected to reopen in Q1 2027. Treat this as planned, not an already operating facility.
- A showroom listing does not prove stock, allocation, service capability or exact model orderability.

## Exclusions / lifecycle watchlist

- **Spectre Series II:** expected in India by end-2026 according to October reporting; watchlist only until India launch/order guide.
- **Spectre Black Badge Series II:** global public-road debut / product updates are not proof of Indian retail orderability; confirm separately.
- **Wraith and Dawn:** discontinued; do not include as current new-car models.
- **Phantom Drophead Coupé, Phantom Coupé, Silver Seraph, Ghost first generation and older Cullinan Series I:** historical/discontinued; exclude from current new-car catalogue.
- **Boat Tail, Droptail, Sweptail and other coachbuilt commissions:** one-off / ultra-limited commissioned cars, not standard current catalogue models. Do not create generic retail variants.
- **Bespoke collections and one-off commissions:** record as special editions only when a current India allocation, official name and price are confirmed.
