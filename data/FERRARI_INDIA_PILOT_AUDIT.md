# Ferrari India — current model and price audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** India  
**Status:** Research workpaper only — not approved for catalogue import or publication  
**Repository:** `feature/catalogue-source-audit-ingestion`

## Executive outcome

Ferrari India requires stricter treatment than mass-market catalogues: model availability and prices are often dealer/order-led, public sources conflict, and several displayed figures are anticipated India–EU FTA booking estimates rather than settled prices.

- Treat the current India retail candidate set as **Amalfi, Amalfi Spider, 296 GTB, 849 Testarossa, 12Cilindri Coupe, 12Cilindri Spider and Purosangue**, subject to dealer confirmation of orderability and delivery terms.
- The India-market launch of **Amalfi Spider** is reported at **₹4.60 crore ex-showroom** (July 2026).
- **Amalfi ₹4.08 crore, 12Cilindri Coupe ₹5.89 crore, 12Cilindri Spider ₹6.38 crore and Purosangue ₹7.59 crore** are reported reduced/anticipated booking figures linked to the future India–EU FTA. They must not be represented as currently effective standard retail prices.
- **296 GTB** and **849 Testarossa** have reported India price observations of ₹5.40 crore and ₹10.37 crore respectively, but these are secondary-source observations, not an OEM price list.
- Ferrari Luce was shown in India in October 2026; no India launch date or India price was announced. Keep it in a watchlist, not the current catalogue.
- Do not import historic models such as Roma, SF90 Stradale, F8 Tributo or 812 merely because aggregator pages still display them.

## Market-entry and lifecycle watchlist

- **Ferrari Luce:** first all-electric Ferrari, unveiled/displayed in India at Universo Ferrari in October 2026. India price and launch date not announced. Watchlist only.
- **296 Speciale / 296 Speciale A:** Ferrari global model-changeover material identifies these as 2026 phase-in models. No verified India retail availability or India price captured; watchlist only.
- **849 Testarossa Spider:** global model announced; no India-specific orderability or price verified. Watchlist only.
- Do not infer Indian availability from global configurator presence or an India exhibition appearance.

## Exclusions / data hygiene

The following appear in Indian aggregator catalogues but are not accepted as current India retail models by this audit: **Roma, SF90 Stradale, F8 Tributo and 812**. They may be historical, discontinued, residual dealer inventory or stale catalogue entries. Require an authorised Ferrari dealer confirmation and current orderability evidence before reinstating any of them.

Do not merge the two Amalfi body styles. Do not merge the 12Cilindri Coupe and Spider. Keep petrol and plug-in-hybrid powertrains distinct. Ferrari options and personalisation can materially alter transaction prices; base price must remain separate from configured vehicle price.

## Import gate / unresolved items

- Obtain written, dated confirmation from an authorised Ferrari India dealer for each model's current orderability, standard ex-showroom price, price validity and whether an FTA-linked quote is conditional.
- Obtain official price documentation before marking any amount as OEM-verified.
- Capture city-specific on-road quotes only when the quote date, city/RTO, insurance, TCS and optional equipment are explicit.
- Confirm source licensing and permitted reuse before copying source content into the public catalogue.
- No Firestore import or publication is authorised by this audit.

**Disposition:** Model candidates may be retained as *research-only*. All price records remain unapproved for production ingestion until dealer/OEM verification and price-class reconciliation.
