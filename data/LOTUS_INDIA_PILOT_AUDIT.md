# Lotus India pilot audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** India  
**Decision:** Lotus India market presence and three current model families are supported by the India-specific official website and current independent market listings. Exact OEM India variant prices and retail orderability still require confirmation.

## Current model candidates

- **Eletre** — electric performance SUV.
- **Emeya** — electric hyper-GT / four-door GT.
- **Emira** — petrol sports car.

The India-specific Lotus site currently presents Eletre, Emeya and Emira in its model navigation and provides India-market pricing disclaimer language. Evija appears in global/heritage promotional material, but no current India retail availability or price was confirmed; keep it out of the current catalogue pending local orderability.
