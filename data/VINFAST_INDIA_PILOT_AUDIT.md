## Price observations

The current OEM homepage exposes model-level "Starting at" observations:
- VF 6: ₹17.29 lakh.
- VF 7: ₹21.89 lakh.
- VF MPV 7: ₹24.49 lakh.

A current independent India model guide reports higher starting figures for VF 6 (₹18.19 lakh) and VF 7 (₹22.99 lakh), and variant-level price ranges. This conflicts with the OEM homepage display. Keep both source observations with their retrieval timestamps and price labels; do not overwrite or blend them. The OEM homepage figures are model-level promotional starting prices, not a complete exact variant price matrix. Confirm whether benefits, limited offers, battery subscription or other price conditions explain the discrepancy before using them as base ex-showroom prices.

The OEM homepage also reports:
- VF 6 battery 59.6 kWh; claimed 468 km ARAI range.
- VF 7 battery 70.8 kWh; claimed maximum range 532 km.
- VF MPV 7 battery 60.13 kWh; claimed 517 km ARAI range.
Preserve test-cycle labels and do not compare ARAI and WLTP figures as equivalent.
