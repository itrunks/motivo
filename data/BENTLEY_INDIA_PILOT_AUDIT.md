## Executive outcome

Bentley’s official India website currently promotes **Bentayga Extended Wheelbase, Flying Spur and Continental GT**. Recent India market reporting identifies six products in the local range: **Bentayga, Bentayga EWB, Continental GT, Continental GT Convertible, Flying Spur and Torcal**. The difference is material: the website's retail navigation is narrower than the reported full range, while third-party catalogue sites disagree on which models are currently orderable.

Bentley confirmed on 19 October 2026 that **Bentayga and Bentayga EWB** are covered by the India–UK FTA tariff provisions. The company did not announce a revised retail price. Earlier dealer-source reporting estimated:
- Bentayga SWB: ₹3.75 crore ex-showroom
- Bentayga EWB: ₹4.20 crore ex-showroom

Those are estimates, not confirmed Bentley MSRPs. The latest October 2026 company statement confirms eligibility, but still does not disclose the price reduction. Do not stage ₹3.75 Cr or ₹4.20 Cr as effective retail prices.

The FTA tariff benefit does not immediately apply to the plug-in-hybrid Continental GT and Flying Spur. The 2026 FTA coverage says the first five years exclude hybrid, EV and hydrogen vehicles. Bentley's official India page identifies Continental GT and Flying Spur as Ultra Performance Hybrid V8 products.

## FTA eligibility and powertrain implications

- Bentley confirmed on 19 October 2026 that **Bentayga and Bentayga EWB ICE models** are covered by India–UK FTA provisions.
- The FTA provides phased tariff reductions subject to quotas and vehicle-origin requirements. Eligibility is not itself a retail price announcement.
- The 28 July Autocar report estimated Bentayga EWB at ₹4.20 Cr and SWB at ₹3.75 Cr, based on expected tariff changes; it explicitly said Bentley had not confirmed exact prices.
- Continental GT and Flying Spur in India are identified as V8 plug-in hybrids. The FTA rules reported in June 2026 exclude hybrids, EVs and hydrogen vehicles from tariff concessions during the first five years.
- Do not assume all Bentley models qualify simply because the brand is British. Vehicle manufacturing origin, powertrain, quota and tariff rules matter.

## Official model and specification notes

- **Bentayga EWB:** Official India page describes a 4.0-litre V8 and extended wheelbase for additional rear-seat legroom. Autocar India reports a single 4.0 V8 petrol variant in its India catalogue.
- **Flying Spur:** Official India page describes the Ultra Performance Hybrid V8 with 782 PS and 1,000 Nm.
- **Continental GT:** Official India page describes the fourth-generation Ultra Performance Hybrid V8 with 782 PS and 1,000 Nm.
- **Continental GT Convertible:** India range report uses this as a separate body style. Verify whether Bentley India names it Continental GTC in the current retail order guide.
- **Bentayga SWB:** Dealer-source FTA reporting estimates a potential India price, but no current official India order guide was found in this audit.
- **Torcal:** The model name appears in third-party India catalogue coverage as an expected mid-2027 launch; no official India product specification or price was accepted.

## Lifecycle / watchlist

- **Torcal:** Mid-2027 expected launch in aggregator reporting; treat as upcoming watchlist only, not current retail stock.
- **Bentayga Speed / Continental Supersports:** global special variants; no India-specific current orderability or price confirmed in this audit. Do not add on global availability alone.
- **Mulsanne, Brooklands, Arnage, Azure, older Continental GT generations and first-generation Bentayga:** historical/discontinued. Exclude from current new-car catalogue.
- **Bentayga Hybrid:** historic global variant; no current India orderability established. Do not include as a current India powertrain.
- **Mulliner bespoke editions:** special commissioned vehicles should be represented as limited editions only when a current India allocation and model identity are confirmed. The 2024 Opulence Edition was a five-car India-specific collection, not a separate model family.
