### Kushaq — current official powertrain/grade matrix

The official Kushaq page currently lists:
- Classic+ 1.0 TSI MT/AT
- Signature 1.0 TSI MT/AT
- Sportline 1.0 TSI MT/AT and Sportline 1.5 TSI DSG
- Prestige 1.0 TSI MT/AT and Prestige 1.5 TSI DSG
- Monte Carlo 1.0 TSI AT and Monte Carlo 1.5 TSI DSG

The page advertises a starting price of ₹10,69,000. This is a model starting price, not the price for each grade/transmission combination.

## Price evidence and import boundary

- Exact official trim prices captured: Kylaq Sportline MT/AT (2 observations) and 2026 Kodiaq grades (3 observations).
- Official model-level starting-price observations: New Slavia ₹9.99 lakh and Kushaq ₹10.69 lakh.
- No complete exact official price matrix was captured for Kylaq, Slavia, Kushaq, Octavia RS or Superb.
- Do not turn model starting prices into variant prices.
- Do not reuse previous-generation Slavia/Kushaq/Kodiaq prices for refreshed model years.
- Store ex-showroom and on-road observations separately. On-road requires city, state/RTO, observation date, effective date and source.
- OEM site states that price and technical details may change without prior notice. Preserve observation timestamps and page footnotes.

## Current / limited-volume / future boundary

- Current OEM catalogue entries: Kylaq, Slavia, Kushaq, Kodiaq, Octavia RS, Superb.
- Octavia RS is a limited-volume / allocation-sensitive product; do not assume continuous availability.
- Superb is promoted on the official India range page; do not classify as upcoming solely from third-party listings, but do not create orderable trim records until official booking and price evidence are available.
- Do not add Fabia, Rapid, Yeti, Karoq, Kodiaq previous-generation grades or other discontinued models to current India inventory.
- Keep future global EV models such as Epiq and Peaq out of current India orderable catalogue unless Škoda India publishes local launch/booking/price evidence.
