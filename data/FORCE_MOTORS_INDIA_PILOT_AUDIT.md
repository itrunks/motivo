# Force Motors India pilot audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** India  
**Decision:** Brand is active, but most official catalogue entries are commercial/shared-mobility products. Gurkha is the clear consumer passenger-SUV candidate.

## Current OEM catalogue and scope

Force Motors' official vehicles page currently exposes:
- Traveller N (passenger vans, school buses, ambulances and delivery vans)
- Urbania DX (premium shared-mobility passenger van)
- Monobus (bus)
- Trax (passenger, school, ambulance and delivery derivatives)
- Special Applications
- Gurkha (off-road SUV)
- e-Traveller Smart Citibus EV

For Motivo's consumer passenger-car catalogue:
- **Include as candidate:** Force Gurkha. Verify current 3-door/5-door body styles, exact trim names and booking status from the dedicated OEM Gurkha site before variant staging.
- **Separate commercial/shared-mobility catalogue:** Urbania, Traveller, Trax, Monobus and e-Traveller. These are passenger-carrying vehicles, but they are not ordinary private passenger cars; do not silently mix them into retail SUV search.
- **Exclude:** school buses, ambulances, delivery vans, troop carriers and special-application vehicles from the consumer catalogue.

## Price evidence

- The official Force Motors vehicle page links to a general Prices section but does not expose a dated Gurkha variant price table in the captured page.
- Independent aggregator snapshots list Gurkha 3-door and 5-door at differing amounts and sometimes confusingly label body-style derivatives as separate models. These are discovery-only and not accepted as OEM price records.
- No exact OEM price observation or city-wise on-road price accepted.

## Identity and variant controls

- Keep Gurkha 3-door and 5-door as body-style/derivative variants under a single canonical Gurkha family unless OEM data defines separate model identities.
- Do not assume the Urbania or Traveller is a retail MPV; preserve its commercial/shared-mobility classification.
- Do not treat Trax Cruiser, Toofan, Citiline and Gama as private passenger-car trims without explicit product-segment classification.
- No future MPV/Viano rumours or unlaunched products in current catalogue.

Gurkha model candidate only; no prices ready for import. Keep commercial vehicles in a separate future category. Source licensing and the exact OEM price evidence remain unreviewed.
