## Executive outcome

The current India retail range is consistently represented by three hybrid model families: **Urus SE, Temerario and Revuelto**. Lamborghini's official global site currently highlights Temerario, Revuelto and Urus SE in its model range, while the official dealer locator confirms authorised showrooms in Bengaluru, Delhi and Mumbai.

- **Urus SE:** ₹4.57 crore ex-showroom, India launch price (9 Aug 2024).
- **Temerario:** ₹6.00 crore ex-showroom, India launch price (reported by Autocar India; launched 30 Apr 2025).
- **Revuelto:** ₹8.89 crore ex-showroom, reported India price.
- Current secondary price listings conflict: CarWale shows ₹4.47 crore / ₹5.87 crore / ₹8.70 crore for Urus SE / Temerario / Revuelto respectively. These are not accepted as OEM prices and should remain separate source observations.
- **Revuelto Roadster** is shown by Autocar India as upcoming for late 2026 with an estimated ₹9–10 crore price. No India OEM launch or confirmed retail price found; watchlist only.
- Do not list Huracán, Aventador, Gallardo or older Urus S/Performante as current new-car models merely because old listings remain online.

## Model identity and specification notes

- **Urus SE:** 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 PHEV. India launch release states 800 CV system output and more than 60 km electric-mode range. It replaced the prior Urus S / Performante retail configuration in the India launch context.
- **Temerario:** 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 with three electric motors, 920 hp combined output, 8-speed dual-clutch transmission and AWD. It replaces Huracán in Lamborghini's global line-up.
- **Revuelto:** 6.5-litre V12 with three electric motors, 1,015 hp combined output, 8-speed dual-clutch transmission and AWD.
- These are distinct canonical model families. Do not model "Urus" generically if the current India derivative is Urus SE; retain the specific SE designation.
- Options, Ad Personam customisation and dealer-fitted equipment can materially change configured price. Keep base price and configured transaction price separate.

## Exclusions / lifecycle watchlist

- **Huracán EVO / STO / Tecnica / Sterrato:** legacy model-family listings remain on aggregators, but Temerario replaced Huracán in Lamborghini's current product transition. Exclude from current new-car catalogue unless a dealer confirms fresh factory orderability.
- **Aventador, Gallardo, Murciélago, Diablo:** discontinued historical models; exclude.
- **Urus S / Urus Performante:** previous Urus variants; current India range is Urus SE. Do not duplicate as current.
- **Revuelto Roadster:** late-2026 upcoming estimate in secondary media; no confirmed India OEM launch, orderability or price. Watchlist only.
- **Revuelto SV / Urus SE Performante / Temerario Tettonero Capsule:** visible on the global Lamborghini homepage, but no India-specific retail availability or pricing was established in this audit. Do not import based solely on global site presence.
