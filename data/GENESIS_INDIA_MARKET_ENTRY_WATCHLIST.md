## Executive decision

**Genesis must not be included in Motivo's current India new-car catalogue at this stage.** The evidence reviewed does not establish an official Genesis India retail launch, India order book, dealer network, current India model configurator, India-specific brochure, or official India price list.

Recent reporting describes a planned 2027 entry and local assembly intent, but exact launch timing, products, specifications, retail channel and prices remain unconfirmed. This is a watchlist record only, not a current product-family or variant dataset.

The old Hyundai Genesis sedan is a different historical product and must not be conflated with the modern Genesis standalone luxury marque.
