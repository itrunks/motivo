## Executive outcome

McLaren's India catalogue currently has three retail model families in recent market coverage: **Artura, GTS and 750S**. The 750S is in its final production phase, with production scheduled to end in 2027 and final orders reportedly being accepted. McLaren is also preparing a broader model-lineup transition, including a future hybrid SUV, but no India launch timing or price is confirmed.

Important price caveats:
- The latest 750S-specific report (9 Oct 2026) gives ₹4.94 crore for Coupe and ₹5.46 crore for Spider before options. It explicitly describes these as revised India prices.
- The GTS price of ₹3.83 crore was reported as an anticipated India–UK FTA-linked reduction in June 2026. McLaren had not publicly confirmed the reduction, and the trade agreement was not yet in force at that report date. Do not treat ₹3.83 crore as a confirmed effective price.
- Artura is listed at ₹5.10 crore by Autocar India. No new dated OEM price circular was found.
- Autocar's model overview continues to display legacy prices (750S ₹7.94–8.78 crore; GTS ₹6.15 crore), which conflict with later reporting of revised prices. Keep observations separate and do not overwrite.
- McLaren India sales are handled through Infinity Cars / McLaren Mumbai. The official retailer's public presence supports the Mumbai retail point, not stock or model-specific orderability.

## Powertrain and specification notes

- **Artura:** 3.0-litre twin-turbo V6 plug-in hybrid, petrol-electric hybrid, dual-clutch automatic. Keep its PHEV identity explicit; do not classify as pure petrol.
- **GTS:** 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 petrol, grand-tourer body style; renamed from McLaren GT in 2024. Preserve GTS as the canonical current model name and GT only as a historical predecessor label.
- **750S:** 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 petrol, 750hp and 800Nm, 7-speed dual-clutch automatic, rear-wheel drive. Coupe and Spider body styles have separate prices and weights.
- McLaren models sold in India are reported as completely built units (CBUs). Do not infer duty benefit or eligibility from engine size alone.

## Lifecycle and future-product watchlist

- **750S:** Production ends in 2027; final orders reportedly being accepted as of 9 Oct 2026. Mark as *final-order / phase-out*, not discontinued yet.
- **Artura and GTS:** Reportedly planned for discontinuation by 2028 as McLaren reshapes its range. This is a reported future plan, not a present discontinuation; keep current until confirmed.
- **McLaren SUV:** McLaren has confirmed development of its first SUV with a hybrid powertrain. No India launch date, final production name, India orderability or price confirmed. Watchlist only.
- **MCL820:** McLaren Mumbai social posts tease a future 820 PS model name for 2027. No official production model name, technical release, India launch or retail price confirmed. Watchlist only; do not treat dealer social teaser as a catalogue record.
- **W1:** Global model presence does not establish India allocation, price or current orderability. Do not add without India-specific evidence.
- **720S, GT, 570S, 600LT, 765LT, Senna, Elva:** historical/discontinued model entries or stale aggregator pages; exclude from current new-car catalogue unless a dealer provides proof of fresh factory orderability. A used/pre-owned listing is not a new-car catalogue entry.

## India retail-channel evidence

- McLaren entered India through retail partner **Infinity Cars**, with the first showroom in Mumbai in late 2022.
- McLaren Mumbai's public retailer profile identifies it as the authorised McLaren dealership in India and links to `https://mumbai.mclaren.com/en`.
- Autocar India reports a McLaren dealer presence in Mumbai. A third-party directory also lists a Mumbai showroom.
- Retailer presence does not confirm a specific model's stock, allocation, build slot or final price.
