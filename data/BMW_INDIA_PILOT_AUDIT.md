## Current and lifecycle checks

- The BMW homepage promotes the New 7 Series, i5 Long Wheelbase, X1 Long Wheelbase, X6 M60i and all-new X3.
- The all-models page also lists separate petrol/diesel variants for X1, X3 and X5.
- BMW Group India announced a price increase of up to 2% effective 2026-07-01 across locally produced and completely built-up BMW and MINI vehicles. Do not apply a flat 2% uplift to old price observations; the live OEM price page is the current observation, and the actual model/variant delta may differ.
- BMW Group India's July 2026 press release names locally produced models including 2 Series Gran Coupe, 3 Series Long Wheelbase, 5 Series Long Wheelbase, 7 Series, X1, X3, X5, X7, M340i and iX1 Long Wheelbase. It lists CBU products including i5 M60, i7, i7 M70, iX, M440i Convertible, M2 Coupe, M4 Competition, M5 and XM.
- Treat locally produced vs CBU as manufacturing/supply classification, not a separate vehicle model.
- Do not import global BMW models (for example iX3, iX5, 2027 3 Series) as current India products without India-specific OEM launch/booking evidence.
- Third-party November 2026 lists contain additional names such as Z4, i4, M8, M4 CS and iX1; retain them as discovery leads only where the official current India page does not expose a matching product card or live configurator.
