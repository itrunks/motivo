# Isuzu India pilot audit

**Audit date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** India  
**Decision:** Active OEM range confirmed. Separate consumer-oriented models from commercial/fleet products; no OEM price matrix accepted.

## Current OEM catalogue

The official Isuzu India all-models page currently lists six product families:
- mu-X
- V-Cross
- Hi-Lander
- S-CAB Z
- S-CAB
- D-MAX

For a consumer car / lifestyle-vehicle catalogue, include as candidates:
- **mu-X** — SUV.
- **V-Cross** — lifestyle pickup.
- **Hi-Lander** — lifestyle pickup derivative, subject to current booking/availability confirmation.

Hold for a commercial-vehicle subcatalogue or exclude from consumer retail search pending scope decision:
- **D-MAX** — base commercial pickup family.
- **S-CAB / S-CAB Z** — commercial single-cab pickup products.

The OEM listing proves catalogue presence, not that every listed derivative is orderable in every state or that each is a passenger-car retail product.

## Current price evidence

- Isuzu official site exposes a GET PRICE lead flow, not a publicly indexed, dated national variant price table.
- Autocar India reports the 2026 D-Max V-Cross range at ₹25.50 lakh–₹30.28 lakh ex-showroom, with four 4x4 variants: Z Standard MT ₹25.50L; Z Premium MT ₹25.79L; Z Prestige Standard MT ₹27.00L / AT ₹30.00L; Z Prestige Premium MT ₹27.28L / AT ₹30.28L. This is independent secondary evidence, not an OEM-published price observation. Keep in research notes only; do not stage as official.
- No verified current OEM price for mu-X, Hi-Lander, S-CAB or D-MAX accepted.

## Configuration and identity controls

- 2026 V-Cross update: 4x2 discontinued; all listed variants are 4x4. Do not carry forward prior 4x2 variants.
- Preserve 1.9L diesel powertrain and MT/AT combinations per the current 2026 V-Cross source; do not infer drivetrain combinations for other models.
- D-MAX and V-Cross are related product naming but must not be collapsed into one model record.
- Keep commercial S-CAB variants outside consumer catalogue unless Motivo explicitly adds commercial pickups.
- Do not infer on-road prices from ex-showroom prices.
