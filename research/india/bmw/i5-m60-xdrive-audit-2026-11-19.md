# BMW i5 M60 xDrive India audit — 19 November 2026

## Scope and current availability
- India model: BMW i5 M60 xDrive (standard wheelbase G60 BEV), not the locally assembled i5 LWB.
- BMW India model page currently offers Build & Price and Book a Test Drive; dealer inventory/VIN allocation is not verified.
- India launch: 25 April 2024 as a completely built-up unit (CBU).
- India launch ex-showroom price: ₹1,19,50,000. The current BMW India page continues to list the model, but a fresh November 2026 official price bulletin was not found; treat ₹1.195 crore as the latest confirmed published India price in the evidence collected, not a dealer quote.

## Variant

|---|---:|---|---|
| i5 M60 xDrive | ₹1,19,50,000 (last confirmed BMW India published price, Apr 2024) | Single-speed automatic | Dual-motor electric AWD |

## Tiruchirappalli on-road estimate
- Autocar India (21 Sep 2026): **₹1,27,27,350** estimated on-road, displayed as ₹1.27 crore. Its displayed breakup: ex-showroom ₹1,19,50,000; insurance ₹6,57,250; other charges ₹1,20,100; individual RTO line shown as ₹0.
- CarWale (older estimate): **₹1,25,96,477**, displayed as ₹1.26 crore; breakup includes ₹51,500 registration, ₹4,74,977 insurance, ₹1,20,000 other charges and ₹1,500 hypothecation.
- These are aggregator estimates, not authorised-dealer quotations; the different insurance/registration assumptions explain part of the difference. Confirm final RTO, TCS, insurance and optional packages in a written Trichy quotation.

## Charging
- DC fast charging up to 205 kW; current BMW technical page gives 10–80% in 30 minutes.
- Standard AC onboard charging listed at 11 kW in the current BMW India technical page (0–100% 4 h 15 min).
- BMW India launch release: complimentary BMW Wallbox with installation, up to 11 kW.
- Optional 22 kW AC Charging Professional was mentioned in BMW India launch material; do not present 22 kW as standard onboard AC charging.
- Charging performance depends on charger, battery temperature and state of charge.

## Chassis, steering and brakes
- Adaptive Suspension Professional.
- Integral Active Steering / rear-wheel steering.
- Front double-wishbone suspension; rear five-link suspension with self-levelling air suspension and electronically controlled dampers (technical media data).
- M Sport braking system; India launch material describes M-specific brake equipment.
- M-specific exterior and chassis calibration.
- 20-inch M alloy wheel fitment is reported for India M60 specification; confirm exact wheel/tyre size on VIN/order sheet.

## Safety and assistance
BMW India launch release explicitly lists:
- Six airbags.
- Attentiveness Assistance.
- Dynamic Stability Control (DSC), including Cornering Brake Control (CBC).
- Electric parking brake with Auto Hold.
- Side-impact protection and crash sensor.
- ISOFIX child-seat mounting.
- Electronic vehicle immobiliser.
- Integrated emergency spare wheel under the load floor.

Additional equipment / caveats:
- Driving Assistant Professional (adaptive cruise, lane assistance and related ADAS) and Parking Assistant Professional are described as **optional** on BMW India's model page, not standard fitment by default.
- BMW India model page describes remote automatic parking/unparking and Digital Key Plus.
- Tyre-pressure display is included in a global 2025 BMW technical media specification, but that is not an India-specific current homologation/order sheet. Mark India TPMS fitment **not verified** pending current order sheet.
- No current India Bharat NCAP / Global NCAP rating verified.

## Source reconciliation / caveats
1. India launch press release (25 Apr 2024) is the primary source for India launch price, CBU status, 601 hp, 795 Nm, 81.2 kWh usable battery, 516 km WLTP, six airbags, wallbox, India interior features and 18-speaker/655 W audio claim.
2. Current BMW India technical page is the primary source for the presently displayed technical values: 83.9 kWh battery capacity, 455–516 km WLTP, 205 kW DC, 11 kW AC, 10–80% 30 min, dimensions and 490 L boot.
3. BMW India model page now lists Bowers & Wilkins as 17 speakers / 655 W; launch press release says 18 speakers / 655 W. Speaker count unresolved.
4. Optional 22 kW AC Charging Professional is not the same as standard onboard 11 kW AC charging.
5. Do not merge i5 M60 xDrive (standard wheelbase, CBU, 601 hp) with i5 LWB eDrive35L M Sport (long-wheelbase, locally assembled, different powertrain and price).
6. WLTP range is a laboratory test-cycle figure, not guaranteed Indian real-world range.
