# BMW i7 India audit — 19 November 2026

## Scope and status
- Market: India; facelift launched 11 September 2026.
- Current retail evidence: BMW India press release confirms dealer bookings and October 2026 deliveries.
- Current variants confirmed: **i7 50 xDrive M Sport** and **i7 M70 xDrive**.
- This is a model/variant research audit, not a dealer stock confirmation. Exact VIN-level equipment and current allocation must be confirmed with an authorised BMW dealer.

## Variants and pricing

| Variant | Ex-showroom (BMW India, Sep 2026) | Assembly |
|---|---:|---|
| i7 50 xDrive M Sport | ₹1,95,00,000 | Locally produced at BMW Group Plant Chennai |
| i7 M70 xDrive | ₹2,65,00,000 | CBU |

BMW states that the ex-showroom price excludes road tax, TCS, RTO/statutory fees, local levies and insurance. Bookings are through BMW India dealerships; deliveries were scheduled from October 2026.

### Tiruchirappalli on-road estimates
- Autocar India (Oct 2026 estimate): 50 xDrive M Sport **₹2,07,68,100** (displayed rounded as ₹2.08 crore); M70 xDrive **₹2,82,23,100** (displayed rounded as ₹2.82 crore).
- CarDekho (recent estimate): 50 xDrive approx. **₹2.05 crore**; M70 approx. **₹2.78 crore**.
- These are aggregator estimates, not dealer quotations. Their difference shows that insurance/other charges and estimate assumptions vary. Obtain a written Trichy dealer quotation before treating either as final.
- Older ZigWheels figures use superseded pre-facelift ex-showroom prices (₹2.10cr / ₹2.58cr) and old variant naming; do not use them for the current facelift.

## Powertrain and performance

| Specification | i7 50 xDrive M Sport | i7 M70 xDrive |
|---|---:|---:|
| Propulsion | Fully electric | Fully electric |
| Motors | Dual motor | Dual motor |
| Drive | xDrive AWD | xDrive AWD |
| Max system power | 335 kW / 455 hp | 500 kW / 680 hp |
| Max system torque | 660 Nm | 1,015 Nm in Sport; 1,100 Nm with Sport Boost / Launch Control |
| Net battery | 112.5 kWh | 112.5 kWh |
| WLTP range | Up to 728 km | Up to 686 km |
| 0–100 km/h | 5.5 sec | 3.8 sec |
| Top speed | 210 km/h | 250 km/h |

**Torque-source reconciliation:** BMW India's 11 September 2026 press release and the September 2026 India brochure state 660 Nm for the 50 xDrive and 1,015/1,100 Nm for the M70. Autocar's launch story reports 600 Nm for the 50 xDrive and repeats the M70's Sport/Boost figures. Use BMW India primary data (660 Nm) as the audit value; retain the secondary-source discrepancy in the evidence log.

## Charging
- Up to 250 kW DC: 10–80% in 28 minutes (BMW claim).
- Up to 22 kW AC: 0–100% in about 6 hours (BMW claim).
- BMW India says both variants include a complimentary BMW Wallbox charger with installation, capable of up to 22 kW.
- Charging time depends on charger output, battery temperature/state of charge and site electrical supply. Actual public charging availability in Trichy is not audited in this model sheet.

## Exterior and chassis
- BMW Iconic Glow kidney grille.
- Swarovski crystal headlights; adaptive LED with cornering light and matrix high beam / non-dazzling selective high-beam assistant, claimed illumination range up to 600 m.
- Flush-fitting door handles; revised rear lamps and bumper.
- 50 xDrive M Sport: 20-inch wheel specification shown for M Sport.
- M70: 21-inch M aerodynamic wheels, mixed-size tyres, M-specific front apron, grille graphic, black mirror caps, M rear spoiler and M Sport brakes.
- Adaptive two-axle air suspension and rear-axle steering are part of the i7 equipment story; verify exact India trim/VIN specification where a brochure configuration note applies.
- Sky Lounge panoramic glass roof; illumination with more than 40 LEDs is described as an available specification, not guaranteed standard fitment on every Indian car.

## Cabin, comfort and infotainment
- BMW Panoramic Vision across the lower windscreen area.
- 17.9-inch central display; BMW Operating System X.
- 14.6-inch front passenger screen (BMW India release describes it as standard equipment).
- 31.3-inch 8K BMW Theatre Screen for rear passengers.
- BMW 3D Head-Up Display.
- Bowers & Wilkins Diamond Surround Sound with Dolby Atmos; India brochure describes 35 speakers and 1,965 W.
- Automatic doors with radar-based operation and Soft Close.
- Multifunction front seats; Executive Lounge rear seating. Rear recline geometry up to 43 degrees is described in BMW India release.
- Heating, ventilation and massage seat functions; powered ottoman / rear lounge configuration.
- BMW Individual Merino leather upholstery options; Tartufo and Smoke White named in launch release.
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto; OTA software updates and connected-car functions reported by Autocar India.
- Inductive smartphone charging up to 15 W.
- My BMW App / remote Parking Assistant Professional functionality; remote parking is confirmed by BMW India release.
- Panoramic glass roof with optional illuminated LED pattern.

## Safety and driver assistance
BMW India release confirms:
- Cruise Control and Attentiveness Assistant.
- Parking Assistant Professional with smartphone remote parking.
- Reversing Assistant Professional.
- Driving Assistant Plus: Active Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Steering and Lane Control Assistant, and blind-spot monitoring.
- Adaptive LED / matrix high-beam assistance.
- BMW India current i7 model page additionally describes Steering Assistant, Lane Keeping and Emergency Brake Assistant, Park Assist with 360-degree camera and sensors, and remote parking via My BMW App.

**Airbag count:** Not marked as verified for the 2026 India facelift. Do not carry forward an old-generation count as a confirmed 2026 fitment.
**TPMS:** Not confirmed from the retrieved 2026 India release/brochure. Older India i7 spec sheets list TPMS, but current variant fitment requires confirmation.
**Crash-test rating:** No current Bharat NCAP / Global NCAP rating verified for this India-spec facelift.

## Dimensions and practical data
- 5-seat luxury sedan; 500 L boot capacity is reported by Autocar India.
- Autocar specification listing: 5,391 mm length, 1,950 mm width, 1,544 mm height, 3,215 mm wheelbase; 136 mm ground clearance; kerb weight listing around 2,715 kg. Treat dimensions/weight as secondary-source values until matched to current India homologation sheet.
- Autocar review notes the spare wheel is mounted on the boot floor and reduces usable luggage volume.
