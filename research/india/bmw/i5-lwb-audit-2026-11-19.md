# BMW i5 Long Wheelbase India audit — 19 November 2026

## Audit scope and current retail status
- India model: BMW i5 LWB (G68), locally assembled at BMW Group Plant Chennai.
- BMW India current model page is live, lists a starting price of ₹79,60,000 and offers Build & Price / Request an offer.
- BMW India launch release dated 7 September 2026 confirms the model can be booked through BMW dealerships and deliveries begin immediately.
- Regular retail variant: **eDrive35L M Sport**.
- A limited **eDrive35L M Sport First Edition** was also launched at the same price, limited to 99 units; BMW says all 99 sold out before the official launch. Treat First Edition as a sold-out launch allocation, not an independently orderable current trim.

## Powertrain and performance

| Specification | i5 eDrive35L M Sport |
|---|---|
| Propulsion | Battery electric |
| Motor layout | Single rear-mounted electric motor |
| Drive | Rear-wheel drive (RWD) |
| Motor type | Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (Autocar technical listing) |
| Maximum power | 268 hp (about 272 PS / 200 kW) |
| Maximum torque | 410 Nm |
| Transmission | Single-speed automatic |
| 0–100 km/h | 6.7 sec (BMW India launch claim) |
| Top speed | 195 km/h |
| Battery gross capacity | 81.6 kWh |
| Claimed range | Up to 669 km MIDC |

**Drive-layout source conflict:** V3Cars' variant listing incorrectly labels the model FWD in its variant summary. BMW's rear-mounted single-motor configuration and Autocar's technical listing specify RWD. Audit value is RWD.

## Charging
- Standard onboard AC charging: up to 11 kW.
- BMW Wallbox up to 11 kW, including installation, is included per launch release.
- DC fast charging: up to 160 kW.
- BMW claim: 10–80% in 33 minutes under suitable conditions.
- Charging times vary with charger output, battery temperature and state of charge.

## Dimensions and chassis
- Length: 5,175 mm.
- Width: 2,156 mm.
- Height: 1,520 mm.
- Wheelbase: 3,105 mm.
- 20-inch M alloy wheels; mixed tyre setup: 245/45 R20 front and 275/40 R20 rear (Autocar).
- Adaptive rear-axle air suspension and comfort suspension are standard per BMW India launch release.
- BMW says ground clearance has been increased for Indian road conditions but does not publish the exact figure in the release.
- One-pedal driving via B mode; adaptive recuperation in D mode.
- 50:50 weight distribution is stated by BMW.

## Exterior
- BMW Iconic Glow illuminated kidney grille.
- Adaptive LED headlamps with matrix technology and cornering lights.
- M Sport exterior package; black side skirts and flush-mounted door handles.
- 20-inch M alloy wheels with wider rear tyres.
- LED tail lamps with L-shaped twin-light design.
- Four launch paint colours: Mineral White, Phytonic Blue, Carbon Black, Oxide Grey.
- 5-pattern welcome carpet light.

## Interior, comfort and infotainment
- BMW Curved Display: 12.3-inch driver information display + 14.9-inch control touchscreen.
- BMW Operating System 8.5; Intelligent Personal Assistant with voice interaction.
- BMW Interaction Bar with ambient lighting and integrated controls.
- CraftedClarity crystal-glass controls.
- Veganza perforated vegan upholstery, standard Copper Brown / Atlas Grey dual-tone combination.
- Powered comfort front seats with active ventilation.
- Four-zone climate control.
- Sky Lounge panoramic glass roof with LED light design.
- Bowers & Wilkins surround sound: **17 speakers, 655 W** (current BMW India model page and Autocar review agree).
- Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- BMW Digital Key Plus: compatible smartphone/smartwatch; share access with up to 18 users (BMW India).
- My BMW App remote vehicle status and remote lock/unlock.
- Remote Software Upgrades / OTA.
- Wireless charging trays in front console and rear armrest; USB-C ports.
- Interior camera can capture photos/video/audio and can be accessed through My BMW App.
- Rear-seat tablet mounting / Travel & Comfort system supported; a tablet and holder were specific First Edition package equipment, not a standard rear screen for all cars.

## Rear-seat and luggage practicality
- Extended wheelbase gives generous rear legroom.
- Rear bench is comfortable for two; centre passenger is less comfortable due to raised floor/seat and central tunnel.
- Rear seats do **not** recline.
- No rear window sunshades, soft-close doors, front-passenger boss mode or rear-seat audio controls per Autocar's road test.
- No head-up display according to Autocar review.
- Boot: approx. 500 litres (Autocar review); spare wheel sits on boot floor and materially reduces usable luggage space.

## Warranty and ownership support
BMW India launch release:
- Standard vehicle warranty: 2 years, unlimited kilometres.
- Extended warranty can be purchased from year 3 up to year 6 without mileage limitation; BMW says it can be extended up to 10 years / 200,000 km under applicable package terms.
- High-voltage battery warranty: 8 years / 160,000 km.
- Complimentary 2-year roadside assistance.
- Charging Concierge for route planning around active chargers and payment/activation support.
- Service Inclusive / BMW Secure / extended warranty packages are optional paid products; exact Trichy price not verified.

## Source reconciliation / caveats
1. BMW India 7 Sep 2026 launch release is primary for price, Chennai assembly, 81.6 kWh gross battery, 669 km MIDC, 268 hp / 410 Nm, 6.7 sec, 195 km/h, charging, 8 airbags, ADAS and warranty.
2. BMW India current model page confirms current listed starting price ₹79.60 lakh, 17-speaker / 655 W Bowers & Wilkins, BMW OS 8.5, Digital Key Plus, rear-axle air suspension, matrix adaptive LED, and current feature descriptions.
3. Autocar India launch story and road test independently confirm single eDrive35L M Sport regular trim, RWD, wheel/tyre setup, 17-speaker audio, TPMS and omissions such as no HUD/rear sunshades/reclining rear seats.
4. First Edition sold out; do not treat its tablet/holder and bronze trim as standard equipment on regular M Sport.
5. MIDC range is a test-cycle figure, not guaranteed Indian real-world range.
6. Aggregator Trichy on-road prices vary and include questionable/ambiguous RTO calculations; dealer quote required.
7. V3Cars FWD label conflicts with BMW/Autocar evidence; use RWD.
