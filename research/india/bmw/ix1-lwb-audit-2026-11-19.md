# BMW iX1 Long Wheelbase — India variant audit addendum (2026-11-19)

Scope: current India-market iX1 LWB, exact powertrain/variant identity, official brochure facts, price reconciliation, safety and equipment evidence. This is a research record, not a production import approval.

## Canonical India variant

- Model: BMW iX1 Long Wheelbase (U11 India LWB)
- Variant: **iX1 eDrive20L M Sport**
- Count: one India variant / one powertrain.
- Propulsion: battery electric, single permanently excited synchronous motor.
- Drive: front-wheel drive.
- Transmission: single-speed automatic.
- Power: 150kW / 204hp.
- Torque: 250Nm.
- 0–100km/h: 8.6s.
- Top speed: 175km/h.
- Battery: 66.4kWh (Autocar India); battery usable/gross split not stated in the current India brochure.
- Range: **546km MIDC** in the September 2026 brochure and current BMW India page. Older India brochure and aggregator entries show 531km; do not overwrite the current brochure value with stale listings.
- AC charging: up to 11kW.
- DC charging: up to 130kW; Autocar reports 10–80% in 32 minutes.
- Length: 4,616mm; wheelbase: 2,800mm (BMW India brochure).
- Width: 1,845mm; height: 1,627mm; boot: 490L / up to 1,600L with rear seats folded (Autocar India / BMW India).
- Tyres: 225/55 R18; 18-inch 838M bi-colour M alloys.
- Suspension: comfort suspension, front MacPherson strut / rear multi-link (Autocar; confirm exact India build sheet if required).

## Equipment evidenced by BMW India

- Adaptive LED headlights with cornering function and High Beam Assistant.
- 9 sq ft panoramic glass roof with electric roller blind.
- Comfort Access and BMW Digital Key Plus; compatible phone/watch can lock, unlock and drive; shareable with up to four people.
- Driving Assistant: Lane Departure Warning, Blind Spot Detection, Rear Collision Warning.
- Parking Assistant Plus with Surround View 360-degree camera and Reversing Assistant (last 50m memory).
- BMW OS 9 / Live Cockpit Plus; 10.25-inch instrument display + 10.7-inch central touchscreen.
- BMW ConnectedDrive / My BMW App and BMW OneShop over-the-air upgrades.
- Harman Kardon audio: 12 speakers, 205W.
- Veganza upholstery.
- Rear seat recline up to 28.5 degrees; rear AC vents; 40:20:40 split-folding rear seats.
- Boot 490L, up to 1,600L with rear seats folded.
- Adaptive recuperation and B-mode one-pedal driving.
- 24x7 Charging Concierge described in brochure.
- 8-year / 160,000km high-voltage battery warranty standard; standard vehicle warranty 2 years; extended car warranty up to 10 years optional.
