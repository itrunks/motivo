## 6. India–UK FTA price handling

- June 2026 reports anticipated the following revised prices: 750S Coupe ₹4.94 crore, 750S Spider ₹5.46 crore, GTS ₹3.83 crore. At that time, sources said McLaren had not publicly confirmed the reductions and the FTA implementation was pending.
- The October 2026 Autocar report subsequently repeated ₹4.94 crore and ₹5.46 crore for 750S final orders. Those are the stronger latest references for the 750S.
- For GTS, no equally clear later manufacturer-confirmed revised price was found. Keep ₹3.83 crore as provisional only; retain ₹6.15 crore as historical/pre-revision catalogue price.
- Artura is a PHEV and was reported not to receive the same initial FTA reduction as >3.0L pure-petrol models.
- All McLaren India vehicles are CBUs. Import quota, country of origin, model year and dealer allocation can affect price and timing. Keep old list price, reported revised price and written dealer quote in separate fields.

## 7. Discontinued, superseded and future models

- GT: superseded by GTS (renamed/facelifted evolution); do not count GT as a separate current India new-car model. Historic portal pages remain.
- 720S: predecessor to 750S; discontinued, not current new-car inventory.
- 765LT / 765LT Spider, 600LT, 570S and older Sports Series: historical/discontinued; no current India orderability evidence.
- Artura Spider: exists globally, but no current India retail listing/orderability was confirmed in this audit. Keep as global derivative / India availability unverified, not as a current India variant.
- W1: global flagship hypercar; no current India allocation/orderability evidence confirmed.
- McLaren's first SUV is under development, but no confirmed production name, India launch timing, price or specifications were found. Keep as future programme only, not a catalogue model.
- McLaren has indicated Artura and GTS will be phased out by 2028; do not mark them discontinued today. The 750S production end is 2027, but it remains orderable while final allocations last.
