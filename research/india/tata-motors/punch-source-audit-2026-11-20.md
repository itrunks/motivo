Exact trim applicability is not stated in the marketing page and must be checked in the current variant matrix.
- The current page describes iTPMS as low-pressure alerts. Do not represent it as individual live tyre-pressure readouts without explicit evidence.
- The 2026 brochure describes twin-cylinder CNG packaging, direct CNG start and thermal/leak protection. Verify current CNG/AMT combinations from a current dealer order sheet.
