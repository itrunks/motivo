## 9. Scope decisions and exclusions

- Count seven current India retail configurations from the revised-price list, across four model families.
- Do not add base Vantage, base DB12, base DBX, DBX707, or earlier DBX707/DB11/DBS as current India retail variants merely because global or pre-owned pages mention them. They need explicit current India orderability evidence.
- Global special models Valour, Valiant, Valkyrie and Valhalla are not included in the India current retail count absent current India price/orderability evidence. Track separately as special/limited/import-on-request candidates.
- DB12 S Volante's official model-page technical output currently surfaces 680PS, whereas DB12 S Coupe is 700PS. Preserve the distinction and mark the India-specific Volante output as requiring confirmation.
- FTA prices are published/reported revised ex-showroom prices; eligibility and actual transaction price can depend on import batch, origin documentation, customs treatment and dealer allocation. Do not calculate on-road prices without state/city tax and insurance inputs.
