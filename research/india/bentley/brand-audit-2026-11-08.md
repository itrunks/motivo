## 2. Bentayga Extended Wheelbase (EWB) — India retail candidate

- India retail candidate: Bentayga EWB 4.0 V8 Petrol. Autocar's current catalogue shows one Bentayga variant; the India website promotes the EWB specifically.
- Price: last recorded India price before FTA was ₹5.90 crore before options. Autocar dealer-source report estimates around ₹4.20 crore after India–UK FTA, but explicitly says Bentley India has not confirmed the exact reduced price. Treat ₹4.20 crore as a provisional market estimate, never a confirmed official price or guaranteed customer quote.
- Powertrain: 3,996cc twin-turbo V8 petrol, 550PS (542bhp), 770Nm, 8-speed automatic, permanent AWD.
- Performance: 0–100km/h 4.6s; top speed 290km/h.
- Dimensions: length 5,305mm; wheelbase 3,175mm; width across mirrors 2,222mm; height approx. 1,739mm (height depends on suspension setting/antenna); official local dealer page lists same dimensions.
- Seating: EWB can be configured as 4-seat or 4+1 / 5-seat depending on rear-seat specification. Do not force one seating count for every bespoke order.
- Equipment evidence: Bentley Dynamic Ride 48V active anti-roll, all-wheel steering, adaptive air suspension, rear-seat comfort focus, extensive Mulliner personalisation. Exact India standard equipment and options must be dealer-confirmed.
- Safety: official global systems vary by model year and specification; India-specific airbag count and ADAS standard fitment not verified. Do not infer from generic aggregator checklists.

## 7. FTA and price handling

- India–UK FTA reportedly allows qualifying UK-built pure-petrol vehicles above 3,000cc to receive a reduced import duty within a quota. Autocar reports Bentayga orders at reduced prices but notes Bentley has not confirmed the exact revised price.
- Bentayga EWB estimated at about ₹4.20 crore; standard-wheelbase Bentayga estimated at about ₹3.75 crore. These are press/dealer estimates, not official manufacturer prices.
- Continental GT and Flying Spur PHEVs are not eligible for the immediate FTA reduction under the reported rules.
- Keep old ex-showroom price, estimated post-FTA price and written dealer quote as separate fields. Do not overwrite historical price with estimate.
- Confirm the vehicle's UK origin, quota eligibility and import batch before using the reduced-duty price.
