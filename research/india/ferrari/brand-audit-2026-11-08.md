## 11. India price and source-confidence rules

- Ferrari vehicles are fully imported; price varies materially with personalisation, optional equipment, import timing, exchange rates and applicable customs duty.
- The India–EU FTA reported future tariff reductions are not the same as current unconditional ex-showroom prices. Autocar reports Purosangue booking prices around ₹7.59 crore with delivery after the agreement takes effect (expected 2027/28); do not blend that with prior ₹10.50 crore price.
- For 12Cilindri, some aggregator figures differ by more than ₹2 crore; classify as Conflict, not average them.
- For 296 GTB/GTS, Autocar and CarWale prices differ; classify as Conflict.
- Keep official Ferrari technical data separate from India-market price/orderability evidence.
- Global safety/ADAS availability is not proof of standard India fitment. Exact India airbag counts, ADAS standard/optional, warranty and service schedules require dealer/build-sheet documentation.
